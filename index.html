How it works

Ask in plain English.
Approve every order.

Dolios reads GMGN’s live data, does the due diligence, sizes the trade and restates it in one line. Nothing executes until you say yes.

dolios — ~/trading

Safety model

Cunning,
not reckless.

01

Trade guard

Every swap, limit, TP/SL or launch stops for your approval — per exact command. Even /yolo can’t skip it.

02

Due diligence first

Names resolve to the right contract among copycats, then liquidity, honeypot, rug ratio, bundlers and insiders are checked.

03

Your keys stay home

No seed phrases, ever. A local signing key authorises GMGN orders; Dolios never holds your funds.

04

No autopilot by default

Scheduled runs can research all night but can’t trade unless you explicitly turn on trade_autopilot.

Install Dolios

One command. Python, Node.js and dependencies go into Dolios’ own folder.

  1. Run the command, pick your AI model provider.
  2. Type dolios and say “connect my GMGN account”.
  3. Ask for the trenches. Approve what you like.
