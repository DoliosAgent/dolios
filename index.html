      Safety model

      Cunning, not reckless.

      01

      Trade guard

      Every swap, limit, TP/SL or launch stops for your approval — per exact command. Even /yolo can’t skip it.

      02

      Due diligence first

      Names resolve to the right contract among copycats; liquidity, honeypot, rug ratio, bundlers and insiders are checked.

      03

      Your keys stay home

      No seed phrases, ever. A local signing key authorises GMGN orders; Dolios never holds your funds.

      04

      No autopilot by default

      Scheduled runs can research all night but can’t trade unless you explicitly turn on trade_autopilot.
